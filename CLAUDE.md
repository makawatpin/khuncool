# khuncool

## Promo videos (`promo/`)

Any request to make or change a promo / ad / TikTok / Reels video for
khuncool.com: work in `promo/` (a separate Remotion project, excluded from
the site's build) and follow [promo/DESIGN.md](promo/DESIGN.md) so every
video keeps the same colours, layout, motion, voice and music. Build new
videos on the shared engine (`promo/src/Reel.tsx`) and copy the structure of
`promo/src/duck/DuckRace.tsx`. Commands are in [promo/README.md](promo/README.md).
